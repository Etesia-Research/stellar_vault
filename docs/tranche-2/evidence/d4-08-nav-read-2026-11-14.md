# D4-08 NAV read, 2026-11-14

Shows that the flagship vault's Blend v2 yield accrues into the vault's NAV. NAV (net asset value) here is the vault's `total_assets()`: every holding valued in USDC by the vault contract itself. Raw responses, the decomposition and the scripts are in [`d4-08-nav-read-2026-11-14.json`](d4-08-nav-read-2026-11-14.json).

## Method

Read-only `simulateTransaction` calls to `https://soroban-testnet.stellar.org` (the RPC method Stellar documents for reading contract functions without submitting); nothing was signed or submitted. Each read calls, on the flagship vault `CD73M44EYNZOX7YWPSBMITVPERWNA6ZHDLAZ2XSM4RQM6V4XT7GYXFQH`, `total_assets`, `convert_to_assets` (1,000 shares), `total_supply`, `state` and `holdings`; on the Blend pool `CCEBVDYM…44HGF`, `get_reserve(USDC)` and `get_positions(vault)`; and on the vault's price oracle `CC3ASLC6…X5EB`, `mark` for tRESERVE and XLM. A read is kept only when all nine calls report the same ledger; the close time comes from `getLedgers`. Contract ids are from the testnet manifest `deploy/testnet/pre-2026-12-16-reset.json` in etesia-vault. The total is then recomputed with the vault's own formula (`pricing.rs` `equity_of`: amount × oracle price / 10¹², USDC at 1), and the Blend position as bTokens × b_rate / 10¹² (b_rate is Blend's USDC value of one bToken, scaled by 10¹²).

## Arithmetic

1. Blend accrual: 829,196,487 × 3,041,608 / 10¹² = 2,522.09, against a measured +2,522 (floored reads).
2. `total_assets` at each read equals the recomputed sum of the holdings exactly (difference 0 at A and at B).
3. Δ`total_assets` = Blend +2,522 + XLM price −1,855,243 + USDC 0 + tRESERVE 0 = −1,852,721, which is the measured change. The XLM term is the oracle mark moving at a constant XLM balance; with it removed, the change in NAV is +2,522, the Blend accrual to the unit.
4. Share price: `convert_to_assets` is `total_assets` × shares / supply, where the supply includes management-fee shares accrued up to the read (1% a year; 251,598,194 share units over 740 s; no performance fee, as NAV is below the high-water mark). Recomputed this way it matches both reads exactly. At A's supply, B's NAV gives 9,986,563,307: −1,727,958 from NAV (Blend +2,352, XLM −1,730,310), −2,343 from the fee accrual.

**Conclusion.** Over 740 s with no keeper activity, the vault's `total_assets()` rose by exactly the 2,522 base units that its Blend v2 USDC position accrued, with the only other change being the XLM price move.
